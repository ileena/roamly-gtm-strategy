# Pricing & Packaging Recommendation — Roamly Groups

> Module 5 · Increase Revenue Through Smarter Pricing and Packaging — ★ Deliverable 5
>
> Propose a pricing model and packaging structure for a product with no existing price. Work in order: value first, packaging second, research last — then compile a one-page recommendation for stakeholders.

## 1. Pricing goal

- **Goal:** Revenue Optimization
- **Why this is the right commercial goal now:** Roamly already has an established presence across 40 cities and an existing customer base. Roamly Groups creates additional value for this known audience through group booking, split payments, and shared itinerary planning, so the initial pricing goal should be to maximize revenue from that additional value.

## 2. Pricing model

- **Model:** Transaction-Based Pricing
- **Who pays — and for what?** Each traveler in the group pays a transaction fee when participating in a group booking. The fee is tied directly to the value moment: successfully booking and paying for the group trip.

---

## 3. Packaging approach

- **Structure:** Single offering with transaction-based pricing
- **Audiences & fit:** Roamly Groups currently serves one primary audience: group travelers, particularly the friend organizing the trip. A single offering keeps the experience simple while giving every traveler access to the core group features.

| Tier | Who it's for | Price | What's included |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Roamly Groups ★** | Group travelers | Transaction fee per traveler | Group booking, split payments, shared itinerary planning, and access to vetted local experiences |

A single offering is recommended rather than multiple tiers because the core value comes from the group using the coordination experience together. Introducing feature-based tiers too early could add unnecessary complexity before willingness to pay is validated.

## 4. Research to validate

- **Method:** Van Westendorp
- **Assumption to validate:** Travelers are willing to pay a transaction fee for the convenience of coordinating group booking, split payments, and shared itinerary planning in one place.
- **The one thing that would change everything if you got it wrong:** The acceptable transaction fee range. If travelers do not perceive enough additional value to pay a fee, the pricing model or who pays would need to be reconsidered.

## 5. The one-page recommendation

> We recommend **transaction-based pricing with a single offering for all group travelers** for Roamly Groups, to **optimize revenue from Roamly's existing customer base**, because the fee is directly connected to the moment when travelers receive value from successfully coordinating and completing a group booking.

For each stakeholder, what they most need to hear:

- **Finance (margin & revenue model):** Revenue scales with completed group transactions and the number of participating travelers, without depending on recurring subscriptions.

- **Sales Leadership (it closes deals):** A simple transaction-based model is easy to explain because customers pay when they actually use Roamly Groups to complete a group booking.

- **Product Marketing (the story that lands):** Pay when you travel together — no subscription required. Roamly Groups makes planning, booking, and splitting payments easier for the whole group.

**Validate first:** Test willingness to pay using Van Westendorp research with the target audience to identify an acceptable transaction fee range before setting the final price and rolling it out broadly.
