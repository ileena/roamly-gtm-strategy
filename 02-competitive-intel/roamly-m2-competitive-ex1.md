# Roamly Groups · Competitive Battlecard & Strategic Bet, Module 2 (Exercise 1)

## Responses
- **Competitor’s name:** Airbnb
- **Their core offering:** A global marketplace for booking stays and local travel experiences.
- **How they position themselves:** An all-in-one travel marketplace for unique stays and local experiences.
- **Their primary audience:** Leisure travelers, families, couples, and groups looking for stays and travel experiences.
- **Known pricing or packaging:** Transaction-based pricing through service fees charged on bookings.
- **Why they’re your biggest threat:** Airbnb already has a large traveler base and offers both stays and experiences, making it easy for groups to use a platform they already know and trust.
- **Looking at this battlecard, what is the one thing that makes you want to go back and adjust your M1 strategy, and why? “I want to revisit [x] because”:** I want to revisit our differentiation because Airbnb already has strong brand recognition and group features. Roamly should focus more clearly on solving group coordination through split payments and shared itinerary planning.
