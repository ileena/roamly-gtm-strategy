# Roamly Groups · Competitive Battlecard & Strategic Bet, Module 2 (Exercise 2)

## Responses
- **Who is Roamly Groups actually for, based on everything you now know?:** Groups of friends traveling together who need an easier way to coordinate bookings, payments, and trip plans.
- **What is the one thing that makes it worth choosing over the competitor?:** A group-first booking experience that lets each traveler coordinate and pay their share in one place.
- **What is the biggest threat to that position, based on your battlecard?:** Airbnb’s strong brand, large inventory, and existing customer base may make groups choose familiarity over better coordination.
- **Of all the signals you’re seeing, which one changes how you think about the market? Hint: looking at how your primary competitor wins, what does that tell you about what Roamly Groups needs to do differently to take that buyer?:** Competing on inventory alone will not beat Airbnb. Roamly needs to win by solving the coordination problems that happen between group members before and during booking.
- **Complete the sentence: “I now believe that Roamly Groups wins when because”:** I now believe that Roamly Groups wins when group coordination matters more than marketplace size, because it makes booking, splitting payments, and planning easier for everyone.
- **“I am betting that Roamly Groups wins by”, your strategic commitment, not what you’re building but how you’ll win with it.:** I am betting that Roamly Groups wins by owning the group travel coordination experience rather than competing with larger platforms on inventory.
- **“To make this bet, I am deprioritizing”, every strategic bet means giving something up. Naming the sacrifice is the proof a real decision was made.:** To make this bet, I am deprioritizing competing on marketplace size and expanding inventory purely to match larger travel platforms.
