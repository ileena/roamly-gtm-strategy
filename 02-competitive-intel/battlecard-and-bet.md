2. Battlecard
- Our one-line advantage: Roamly Groups brings group booking, split payments, and shared trip planning into one coordinated experience.
- Trap to set / question to plant: How does your group currently coordinate the booking, collect everyone's payment, and keep the trip plan updated?
- Objection → response:
  - "We already use Airbnb." → Airbnb is strong for booking travel, but Roamly Groups is designed around coordinating the whole group.
  - "We can just use WhatsApp and send each other money." → You can, but that means managing the trip across multiple tools. Roamly keeps the group journey in one place.
3. The strategic bet
We are betting that groups will choose a better coordination experience over a larger marketplace when planning and paying together becomes difficult.

- Why now: Group travel is already coordinated digitally, but planning, booking, communication, and payments are still fragmented across different tools.
- What we're deliberately NOT doing: Competing with large travel platforms on inventory size.
- What would prove the bet right within 90 days: Higher group-booking completion, strong split-payment adoption, and repeat use of shared itinerary planning.
4. Evidence
- Airbnb's biggest advantages are scale, inventory, brand recognition, and an existing traveler base.
- The opportunity for Roamly is not to beat Airbnb on scale, but to solve group coordination better.
- Group travelers often need to coordinate decisions, payments, and plans across multiple people and tools.
- Roamly Groups combines group booking + split payments + shared itinerary planning around that specific problem.
